Privacy Policy

App: Arlanda Taxikö Bevakning
Developer: SnabbTech (Humair Rasool)
Last updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the app "Arlanda Taxikö Bevakning" (the "App") handles your information. Your privacy matters to us, and the App is designed to work without collecting any personal data.

Information you enter in the App

To use the App, you enter your car registration number. This information is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us, to any server, or to any third party. We cannot see it, and we do not collect, share, or sell it. You can remove it at any time by editing it in the App, clearing the App's data in your device settings, or uninstalling the App.

Information we do not collect

  • The App does not require you to create an account or log in.
  • The App does not access your location.
  • The App does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or any other personal data.
  • The App does not use advertising or analytics services.

Internet connection

The App may use your device's internet connection to display up-to-date queue information. Your car registration number is not sent as part of this.

Third parties

We do not sell, trade, or share your data with third parties. Your registration number never leaves your device.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
